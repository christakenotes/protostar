01 The Concept
FVG
Fair Value Gap — a 3-candle imbalance where price moved too fast, leaving an unfilled gap in order flow. Candles 1 and 3 do not overlap.
IFVG
Inverse Fair Value Gap — two opposing FVGs that overlap in price space. A bullish and bearish gap intersecting, creating a zone of reclaimed imbalance.
A+ Signal
The IFVG is only valid if a liquidity sweep occurs before price enters the overlap zone. No sweep = no signal. The sweep is what anchors it to real institutional footprint.
The Edge
After a directional move, prior FVGs in the opposite direction become unmitigated gaps. When price returns, the imbalance acts as a magnet and then a launch pad.

02 Execution — Step by Step
01
Confirm Your Session
Before you even look at price, check the clock. This setup only trades Asia and London sessions. NY is filtered out — the data is clear on this. 30.6% win rate in NY versus 58–60% in Asia and London. That is not a coinflip difference. That is a different market.
✓ Asia · 12 AM – 3 AM ET ✓ London · 3 AM – 8 AM ET ✗ NY — skip entirely ✗ Other — no edge
02
Wait for the Liquidity Sweep
Price must take out a prior swing high or low with a wick, then close back in the opposite direction. This is not optional. This is the entire premise — institutions swept liquidity before positioning. The sweep confirms there is real order flow behind the move, not random noise.
Wick through swing high/low → close back opposite direction → sweep confirmed
No sweep = no signal. Skip it regardless of how clean the IFVG looks.
03
Wait for the Indicator to Mark the Zone
Load the Protostar IFVG indicator on your 5m NQ or MNQ chart. When conditions are met — two opposing FVGs overlapping, strong body candle, sweep confirmed and causally linked — the indicator draws the zone automatically. You are not manually hunting these. You wait for the mark.
Zone must appear after the sweep, not before. Causal order is enforced by the indicator.
Also verify: zone width must be ≤ 30 points. Wide zones (30pt+) formed during high-volatility chaos consistently underperform. The filter is built into the indicator settings.
04
Check the Prior FVG is Unmitigated
The indicator handles this automatically, but understand why it matters. If price already closed inside the original FVG zone between its formation and now, the gap is mitigated — the imbalance no longer exists. Trading a mitigated zone is trading a ghost. The indicator will not mark it if it's already closed.
Unmitigated = price has not closed inside the original FVG since it formed.
05
Entry, Stop, Target
Entry is the close of the 5m candle that causes the indicator to mark the zone. Aggressive entry — no waiting, no limit orders, no secondary timeframe. The candle closes, the zone appears, you enter at that close. Stop goes beyond the sweep candle's high or low — the structural point that, if broken, invalidates the entire premise. Hard maximum: 50 points. If your stop needs more than 50 points, pass.
