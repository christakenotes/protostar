04 Key Findings
1
NY Session Is a Different Market
30.6% win rate on 36 signals is not a rough patch. It is a structural difference. NY IFVG signals form in a noisier, more reactive environment where institutional footprint is harder to read. Removing NY takes the system from 47.7% → 59.1% — an 11.4 point jump on win rate alone.
2
Zone Width Is a Quality Signal
IFVG zones wider than 30 points underperform consistently. Wide zones form during high-volatility chaos — the kind where price moves so fast the imbalance is less structural and more random. Filtering zones ≤30pts pushes win rate from 59.1% → 61.4%.
3
SHORT Outperforms LONG in This Period
66.7% SHORT vs 58.6% LONG in the filtered set. Feb–Apr 2026 included significant NQ sell-offs, so this directional edge is partially regime-specific. However, it reinforces the importance of reading macro bias — SHORT IFVGs in bearish conditions perform better than LONG IFVGs fighting the trend.
4
Aggressive Entry Leaves Edge on the Table
All backtest data uses entry on the close of the signal bar — the most aggressive approach. A pullback entry (waiting for price to retrace into the IFVG zone) is untested but expected to improve both win rate and R-multiple by reducing initial risk and improving fill quality. This is the next development phase.
5
This Is Backtest Data, Not Live Data
107 signals over 3 months. Structured, filtered, honest. But it is historical. Forward testing is ongoing. The system is production-ready — the edge is documented. Live results will be logged publicly in the Singularity Discord as they happen.
